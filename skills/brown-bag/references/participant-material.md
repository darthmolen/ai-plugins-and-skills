# Participant material template

The handout. Derived from the approved presentation outline. It must make sense to someone who
missed the talk.

```markdown
# <Talk title>

<One paragraph: what this covers and what you will be able to do after reading it.>

## Before you try anything

- [ ] <step that prevents lost work or a data leak>
- [ ] <another, if any>

## <Segment 1 name>

<The points from this segment, written for a reader rather than a listener. Keep `[S]`/`[P]`
markers where the distinction matters to the reader.>

**Try it:**

    <command or prompt to type>

## <Segment 2 name>

...

## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <term> | <one-line definition> |

## Read more

- <source document title> — <link or path> — <what it adds beyond the talk>

## Notes

&nbsp;
```

## Rules

- Commands and prompts are copied exactly as shown in the talk, in code blocks.
- The pre-flight checklist comes before the first instruction it protects.
- Each term is defined once, in the Terms table; the body uses it without redefining it.
- Link to the source documents, not to the slides. The sources stay current; the slides do not.
